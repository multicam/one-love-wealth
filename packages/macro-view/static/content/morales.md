# Isaiah Morales: The Liquidity Pulse

## Core Thesis
Capitulation is emotional; liquidity is mechanical. Using ML models to quantify "wall of worry" setups where liquidity diverges from sentiment.

## Key Insights
- **LP Model**: Blending repo, M2, and credit spreads into a sigma-normalized score.
- **Asymmetric Returns**: Buying extreme fear when the Liquidity Pulse is positive has an 89% historical win rate.
- **RICK/GOLD Ratio**: A proxy for consumer disposable income and risk appetite.

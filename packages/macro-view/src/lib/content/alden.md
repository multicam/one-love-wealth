# Lyn Alden: Fiscal Dominance & Debasement

## Core Thesis
In an era of high debt and fiscal dominance, Bitcoin serves as the premier "long asset, short fiat" play and a global liquidity barometer.

## Key Insights
- **M2 Correlation**: Bitcoin has a 0.94 correlation with global M2 supply.
- **Debasement Hedge**: Structural inflation makes sovereign bonds a poor store of value compared to digital gold.
- **Network Effects**: Bitcoin's moat is its self-reinforcing security and adoption.
